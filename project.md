# coil-tpde

## Summary

A port of the TPDE compiler back-end framework to Coil, generic over the IR it
compiles, and then the fast (-O0-class) native backend for the Coil compiler.
See [docs/PLAN.md](docs/PLAN.md) for decisions, gates and milestones, and
[docs/BENCH.md](docs/BENCH.md) for the scorecard.
